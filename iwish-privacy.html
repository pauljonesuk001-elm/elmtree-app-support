App support & privacy

iWish for.. privacy

Updated 19 November 2026. App operator: Paul Jones, using the Elmtree project name.

Scope

This notice describes the new phone-held iWish implementation. It does not describe the older privately distributed TestFlight Build 24, which used hosted family accounts. Existing test records have not automatically been moved or deleted. Earlier-version testers can use support to ask about those records.

Your family’s phones

Family profiles, wishlists, gift-buying markers, parent sign-in verifiers, private keys and pending changes are held in encrypted storage on authorised phones. Children connect using a parent-issued code, a matching check and explicit approval. A parent can disconnect a device. Children cannot issue invitations, use buying links or chat with other users.

The new parent username and password protect the parent screen on that phone. They do not create a global hosted account. Keep the separate password-recovery key. Replacing a lost phone requires an encrypted family recovery copy and its separate key, saved by the family. App support cannot recreate a missing file or key.

Sharing between houses

Encrypted changes pass through a live Supabase Broadcast relay. The replacement does not write family lists into the developer’s hosted family database. Delivery queues and saved list copies stay on the phones. The relay processes opaque routing identifiers, encrypted packets and normal connection metadata; we do not claim that using the internet creates no metadata.

When background notification delivery is configured, Expo and Apple process device-routing tokens and a wake-only notification. The wake message contains no family name, child age, wishlist or wish. Expo stores notification-routing information and the developer’s notification credential. iOS may delay background activity. A pending change stays on its sending phone until delivery is confirmed.

Parents approve child connections and choose individual lists to share with invited grown-ups. Purchase markers are kept away from the recipient. Local sign-in and a pairing code are access controls; this notice does not describe them as independent verification of a person’s identity.

Discovery and optional photos

Online discovery sends a shopping country and a search term or product reference to the discovery service and relevant public sources. A search term is entered by the user and could itself contain personal information, so please avoid adding family details. The request does not attach a family profile, child name, age or list identifier. Retailer websites are separate services; only grown-ups can open buying links.

If you choose to use a photograph in the app, it is part of the phone-held family content or the specific search action you select. The app asks for the relevant device permission. Do not upload photographs containing other people’s personal information without authority.

Retention, deletion and payments

Phone-held records remain until the family deletes them or removes the app. Disconnecting a child device queues a disconnection for that device; a phone that is offline cannot receive the command immediately. Encrypted recovery files saved outside the app remain under the family’s control and must be removed separately if no longer wanted.

iWish has no advertisements, in-app purchases or subscriptions. Apple handles the upfront app purchase. Optional physical-gift purchases happen with retailers outside iWish. Grown-up feedback is optional, kept for up to 90 days, and does not automatically include family records.

Contact and privacy requests

Use the private support form and choose “Privacy or deletion request”. You do not need an email account. Save the reference and access key to read a reply. Do not send a child’s name, age, photograph, wishlist, password or family recovery key.

This contact service stores the message you choose to send, the app and topic, a reference, a hash of the private reply key, and any operator reply. It uses Supabase hosting. Messages are automatically removed within 90 days; deletion can also be requested using your private reference and key. Connection information is processed for delivery and abuse prevention. The support service uses a daily keyed hash of the connection address for rate limiting; the raw address is not written into the support message.

Depending on applicable law, you may request information about personal data held through support, access, correction, deletion or restriction, and may complain to your local privacy authority. Access to a phone-held family cannot be granted through support. We may ask for proof that you control a support ticket before releasing or deleting its contents.

This website

The support website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub records visitors’ IP addresses for security. The site does not add advertising, analytics, social widgets or tracking cookies. Third-party services have their own privacy information: GitHub, Supabase, Apple.
