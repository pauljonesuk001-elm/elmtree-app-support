App support & privacy

WildWorld privacy

Updated 19 November 2026. App operator: Paul Jones, using the Elmtree project name.

Learning on your device

The app does not require an account or a child’s name, email, age, photograph or location. Saved progress, chosen colours and collected cards remain in app storage on the device. WildWorld also stores virtual-pet progress locally. No advertising, in-app purchases, subscriptions, social feed, public profile or user-to-user chat is included.

Maeve read-aloud narration is generated on the device with bundled models. The app does not send learning passages to an online voice-generation service. Optional spoken search asks for microphone and speech-recognition permission and requires on-device recognition; it does not save a recording. You can refuse these permissions and type your search instead.

Delete the app to remove its local app storage. Apple may operate device backup, purchase, download and optional diagnostic services under your Apple settings and Apple’s privacy terms. We do not receive your payment-card details.

Public animal information and photographs

Bundled content works from the app’s installed resources. Some animal guides and additional photo features can request public information from sources such as Wikipedia, Wikimedia Commons and iNaturalist. These requests concern the animal or public source; the app does not attach a child profile, nickname, age or family identifier. The source service receives normal connection information, including the IP address, and operates under its own privacy terms. Public content may be cached on the device. Photo credits identify sources and licences.

Optional grown-up feedback

Settings includes a grown-up gate before the feedback form. The form is optional and asks you not to include personal or family details. Submitted feedback contains your message, category, app version, build number and platform. It goes to Elmtree’s private feedback inbox using Supabase and is retained for up to 90 days. The app does not attach a profile, wishlist, device advertising identifier or child record. The hosting service processes connection information to deliver the request and prevent abuse.

The feedback form in the installed app currently gives a receipt but does not display replies. For a support or privacy request that needs a reply, use the private support form linked on this page. This is contact with the app operator, not chat between app users.

Contact and privacy requests

Use the private support form and choose “Privacy or deletion request”. You do not need an email account. Save the reference and access key to read a reply. Do not send a child’s name, age, photograph, wishlist, password or family recovery key.

This contact service stores the message you choose to send, the app and topic, a reference, a hash of the private reply key, and any operator reply. It uses Supabase hosting. Messages are automatically removed within 90 days; deletion can also be requested using your private reference and key. Connection information is processed for delivery and abuse prevention. The support service uses a daily keyed hash of the connection address for rate limiting; the raw address is not written into the support message.

Depending on applicable law, you may request information about personal data held through support, access, correction, deletion or restriction, and may complain to your local privacy authority. Access to a phone-held family cannot be granted through support. We may ask for proof that you control a support ticket before releasing or deleting its contents.

This website

The support website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub records visitors’ IP addresses for security. The site does not add advertising, analytics, social widgets or tracking cookies. Third-party services have their own privacy information: GitHub, Supabase, Apple.
